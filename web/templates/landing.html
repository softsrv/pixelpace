How to play

Three stages to the finish

  1. 1

    Connect

    Sit down at your rowing machine and pair the PM5 over Bluetooth in a couple of clicks.

  2. 2

    Line up

    Open a room and share its join code, invite a friend, or hit Quick Match. Two to eight rowers per race.

  3. 3

    Row!

    Once everyone is ready, a 10-second countdown starts the race. First across the line wins.

Race features

Everything you need to compete

Race your friends

Create custom rooms, share join codes instantly, and send direct friend invites to line up side-by-side at the start line.

Race strangers

Jump into the action immediately with Quick Match and test your mettle against rowers around the world, matched by your recent finish times.

Badges and achievements

Earn badges and achievements as you log split personal bests, rack up distance, and win head-to-head sprints.

Concept2 Logbook sync

Automatically sync with Concept2 Logbook and ergdata so your verified race workouts count toward your season totals.
